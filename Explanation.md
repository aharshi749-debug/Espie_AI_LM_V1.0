## Story Time

A while back, Elias made an AI called MegaLM, and I really liked the idea behind it.

However, I found that the original system had problems with hallucination and generated text that could become difficult to use reliably.

I decided to take the source, port it, and substantially rewrite several parts of the system, especially the output and generation system, with the goal of making the model more reliable and useful.

That project eventually became Espie. MegaLM was the earlier foundation. Espie is a much larger, substantially rewritten implementation that I developed from that foundation.

## Funny Story

I nicknamed the original MegaLM "HAL-BOT" because of how much it hallucinated.
